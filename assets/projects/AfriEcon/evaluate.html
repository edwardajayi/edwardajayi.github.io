Annotation Quick Guide

Quality Scoring Scales

5
Excellent / Professional Mentions country & year explicitly. Fully self-contained and ready for automated benchmarking.
4
High Quality Clear, accurate, and professional. May have extremely minor phrasing simplicity.
3
Average / Acceptable Correct but uses vague references like "the document" or "this country" without naming them.
2
Poor / Unprofessional Awkward phrasing, poor grammar, or difficult for a human reviewer to understand quickly.
1
Serious Error / Discard Nonsense text, code snippets, or completely broken logic. Must be discarded.

Fact-Checking Guidelines

  • Check that every number in the answer appears in the evidence.
  • Ensure the answer doesn't add external information not found in the text.
  • Mark as Hallucination if any part of the answer contradicts the source.
💡 The Self-Contained Test

If you read the question to someone who hasn't seen the report, would they know exactly which country and year it refers to? If not, the score must be below 5.

General Tips

  • Avoid questions that start with "Based on the text..." or "According to the report..." — these should be scored 3 or lower.
  • Keep observations objective in the notes section.
