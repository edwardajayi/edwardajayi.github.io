Publications

Research Interests: Socially Aware AI, Multimodal AI, Humor Generation and Detection, AI for Mental Health

I work as an NLP Research Associate at the Spatial and Language Technologies Lab at Carnegie Mellon University, focusing on developing AI systems that understand human communication with social awareness, cultural nuance, LLM evaluation, and multimodal ML.
